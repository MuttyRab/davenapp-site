Frequently asked questions

Anything missing? Ask the developer a question or suggest an improvement.

Does Daven work offline?

Yes. Bundled prayer content, calendar logic, saved preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, and core zmanim are designed to work without a network connection. The daily siddur and all 150 chapters of Tehillim are included. Premium adds the specialty and seasonal library.

Which nusachim does Daven include?

Ashkenaz, Sefard, Edot Mizrach, and Chabad. Changing the setting changes the whole library — the services, the blessings, and the text inside them — not just a paragraph here and there.

What does "the siddur that knows what today is" mean?

Daven reads the Jewish calendar. On Rosh Chodesh the morning service carries Hallel and Musaf; on Chanukah, Al HaNissim; on a fast day, Aneinu; on motzei Shabbos, Atah Chonantanu and Havdalah. On a plain Tuesday, none of that appears. Occasion-only passages in every prayer — including bentching — appear only on their days.

Where can I find the weekly parsha?

On Today, look below the Hebrew date. Daven shows the upcoming parsha there when a holiday or calendar event is not taking that line.

You can also open Calendar, select an upcoming Saturday in the month grid, and scroll to the selected date’s details below the grid. The parsha appears there when available; selecting a weekday does not show that line.

Use the Israel schedule switch near the bottom of Calendar for your location: on for Israel, off for the Diaspora.

Is an account required?

No. An account is only for optional cross-device sync of your preferences, bookmarks, and reading progress. Sign in with an emailed link; no password is needed.

What's free and what's Premium?

The daily siddur — all four nusachim, the complete services, and all of Tehillim — is free, forever. Premium adds English translation and transliteration, the specialty and seasonal library, cross-device sync, monthly and weekly Tehillim cycles, a cholim list woven into a Misheberach, calendar sync, prayer export, Prayer Direction, and six side-by-side zmanim opinions.

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is $29.99/year, $3.99/month, or $79.99 as a one-time purchase (US prices). Both subscriptions include a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Your local price and eligibility appear in the app before purchase. All billing is handled by Apple.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app: open Apple's subscription settings (or Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone) and cancel there. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple directly at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple reviews and decides refund requests for all App Store purchases.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Open Settings → Premium → Restore purchases while signed into the Apple ID that made the purchase.

Why is a zman different from my shul calendar?

Zmanim depend on the calculation method and the exact location. Check the selected city and the Israel/Diaspora setting, and try a different opinion (each time in Daven states its method). Then follow your community calendar and local halachic authority — Daven is a practice aid, not a posek.

Does the app work on Shabbos?

By default, Daven shows a rest screen on Shabbos and Yom Tov and returns after the configured end time. This is an adjustable setting in the app; follow your own practice.

Does the tefillin mirror record me?

No. It is a live, on-device camera preview only. Nothing is recorded, stored, or uploaded — ever.

How do I delete my synced account?

Settings → Account → Delete synced account. This permanently removes the account and all synced notes, bookmarks, progress, and preferences. It does not cancel an Apple subscription — manage that in your Apple ID settings.

What data does Daven collect?

As little as possible. No ads, no data sale, no cross-app tracking. Prayer text and personal notes are excluded from anonymous analytics. Optional sync stores the data you choose to sync; location lookups can use your coordinates. Analytics can be turned off in Settings. The full details are in the privacy policy.
