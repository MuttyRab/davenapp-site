What changes in davening on Chanukah?

Say Al HaNissim in the Amidah and Birkat Hamazon, and say full Hallel at Shacharit on all eight days. Here’s where each addition goes.

Chanukah 2026

The first night is Friday, December 4. Chanukah ends after nightfall on Saturday, December 12. See Hebcal’s date-by-date calendar for the candle count each night.

Al HaNissim in the Amidah and bentching

Find Al HaNissim in Modim during the Amidah and in the second blessing of Birkat Hamazon, the blessing for the land. It begins with the evening service at the start of Chanukah and continues through the eighth day.

Full Hallel and the Torah reading

Say full Hallel at Shacharit each day. The Chanukah Torah reading is from Parshat Naso, about the offerings brought to dedicate the Mishkan, the Tabernacle. See Chabad.org’s guide to Chanukah prayers for the details.

When Chanukah overlaps Rosh Chodesh Tevet

Both days of Rosh Chodesh Tevet fall during Chanukah in 2026. Add Ya’aleh V’yavo and say Musaf for Rosh Chodesh. Hallel remains full because it is Chanukah. If you are unsure about the order or your shul’s minhag, follow your community’s practice.
