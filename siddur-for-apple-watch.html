A siddur for Apple Watch

Read today’s tefillah or check the next zman on your wrist. Daven’s Apple Watch app works offline, even when your iPhone is elsewhere.

View Daven on the App Store

What you can open on the watch

  • Now shows a countdown to the next zman.
  • Siddur opens the day’s service in Ashkenaz, Sefard, Edot Mizrach, or Chabad.
  • Brachos and zmanim let you find a blessing or look up the day’s prayer times.
  • Calendar, compass, and Omer put the Hebrew date, prayer direction, and seasonal count on your wrist.

How to install Daven

  • On your watch: Open the App Store, search for Daven: Siddur & Zmanim, and tap Get.
  • On your paired iPhone: If Daven is already installed on your phone, open the Watch app, go to My Watch, then find Daven under Available Apps and tap Install.

Daven requires watchOS 10 or later. After installation, the watch can load its prayers and calculate zmanim on the watch itself. See Apple’s installation instructions if it does not appear.
