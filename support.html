Quick fixes

Premium isn't showing after I paid

Open Settings → Premium → Restore purchases while signed into the Apple ID that made the purchase. If it still doesn't appear, send a message via the contact form with the approximate purchase date.

The times look wrong

Check the city shown above your zmanim, and the Israel/Diaspora setting in Settings. Every zman states its calculation method — choose the opinion your community follows.

Sync isn't working between my devices

Make sure you're signed in on both devices (Settings → Account) with the same account, and that both are online at least briefly. Sync is a Premium feature.

The app shows a rest screen

It's Shabbos or Yom Tov where your device is. The app steps aside on holy days by design and returns after havdalah time.
