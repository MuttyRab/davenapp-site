How to read your local zmanim

Zmanim are the times used for Jewish prayer and observance. They change with your location, the date, and the calculation method.

What the names mean

Latest Shema
The morning deadline for saying Shema. Check whether the time shown follows the Gra or a Magen Avraham calculation.
Chatzot
Halachic midday, which can differ from noon on the clock.
Mincha Gedolah
The earliest standard time for Mincha. It falls after chatzot.
Shkiah and tzeit
Sunset and nightfall. A calendar can show several nightfall times, each based on a different definition.

Why two calendars show different times

First check the location, date, and time zone. Then check the calculation method. For example, the Gra method counts daytime hours from sunrise to sunset, while Magen Avraham calculations use a longer day. Hebcal explains the calculation definitions. Follow the times and customs used by your community.

Find your zmanim in Daven

  1. Open Daven on iPhone and set your location or choose a city.
  2. Open Zmanim to see the day’s times and the displayed calculation methods.
  3. If you travel, check that the displayed location matches where you are.
